2Solve the camera

Find the camera whose rays pass through every landmark's click: focal length, rotation and position, by least squares on the pixel error. A weak pull keeps it near 400 ft out.

u = K (R X + t) minimise Σ ‖project(Xᵢ) − clickᵢ‖² + (3 · (dist − 400 ft) / 40 ft)²

3Read the body on the release plane

Trace each body keypoint back along its camera ray to the vertical plane 3 ft in front of the rubber, then read the angles there, the same way in every stadium.
