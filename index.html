The algorithm

PSI (Pitcher Stability Index) asks one question per outing: is he pitching like his own previous three outings? 100 means yes. The full method (data, pipeline, baselines, validation, references) is on the Method page.

1 · change

d = |change| ÷ threshold

For each part, this outing against his previous 3. The threshold is the smallest change the measurement can detect at 95% (pitch-to-pitch scatter and the number of pitches).

2 · part score

score = 100 · e−d²/4

3 · PSI

PSI = ½ results + ½ mechanics

Each half is the average of its parts. If a half cannot be measured for an outing, PSI is the other half.
