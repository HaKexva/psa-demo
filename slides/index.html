How it works

Five steps, about five minutes per video on a Mac

01

Find the pitching shot

Centre-field frames kept; replays and close-ups skipped.

02

Read the scorebug

Count, pitch count and speed, by macOS text recognition.

03

Track the pose

Apple Vision body pose; a learned picker finds the release frame.

04

Join each pitch

Matched to open play-by-play where it exists; otherwise the scorebug's count changes give the result.

05

Score the outing

PSI: this outing against his own previous three.

PSA · 04

Each step runs locally on a Mac with built-in Apple frameworks. The demo page walks the 2025 黑豹旗 final through all five steps.

The score

PSI: is he pitching like himself?

d = |change| ÷ threshold

Threshold: the smallest change the measurement can detect at 95%. d = 1 is a change just big enough to be real.

score = 100 · e^(−d²/4)

100 with no change, 78 at the threshold, 37 at twice it. Equal to 100 × Harrington's two-sided desirability (1965), n = 2, limits at ±2 thresholds.

PSI = ½ results + ½ mechanics

Results: velocity, command. Mechanics: posture, timing, release consistency.

80+ steady

60–79 worth a look

below 60 flagged

PSA · 05

Dividing by the detection threshold puts km/h, degrees and milliseconds on one scale. The bell curve is flat near zero, so noise costs almost nothing, and it has no cliff at the threshold. Each part score equals 100 times Harrington's two-sided desirability function from quality engineering (1965), exp(−|y′|ⁿ), with n = 2 and the specification limits at plus and minus two detection thresholds, where it scores 1/e, our 37.

Inside one game there is no day-to-day variation, so a sampling-only threshold is right, and the in-game score is calibrated and sensitive. Between games the threshold must include normal variation; our league test showed that, and it is the next fix.

Every stadium is different

The field is the ruler

Each park's centre-field camera sits at a different angle, so 5° of lean in one park is not 5° in another.

13 landmarks on one frame (batter's boxes, plate, rubber), at their official distances, give the camera's direction and zoom.

Cuts of the same broadcast agree within 0.1–0.5°.

PSA · 11

These checks passed. On high-school video the release picker matched every hand label it answered, and the in-game score does not treat noise inside a game as change. The two angle checks come from CPBL broadcasts.

What we can't claim yet

PSI notices change; it does not yet predict

Thresholds too tight

2025 黑豹旗, 10 full games: a pitcher's fastball moves 1.8 km/h between games (SD), not 0.9 as sampling alone predicts; 42% of changes cross a "95%" threshold (CPBL 2023–2025: 56%).

No outcome to test yet

High-school games have no open play-by-play, so only coaches can say whether a flag mattered. In CPBL, a low results score did not predict the next start (ρ = −0.02).

One tournament, 8 pitchers

蔡辰瀧 (平鎮) lost 5.6 km/h over four games, 146 → 140 km/h, with 9 days' rest before the semi-final: a lead for his coach, not a finding.

PSA · 12

On high-school games, as on CPBL, the thresholds leave out normal game-to-game variation: that is the first fix. School games have no open play-by-play, so the outcome test has to come from coaches.
